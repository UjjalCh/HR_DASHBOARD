# HR_DASHBOARD
## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The HR Dashboard provides a clear overview of employee information, workforce demographics, job roles, salaries, experience, job satisfaction, and other important HR metrics. It helps users understand the overall workforce and analyze employee-related data interactively.

## 💍 Marital Status Filter

The Marital Status slicer allows users to filter employee information based on marital status. This helps analyze workforce characteristics across different marital-status groups.

## 👨‍💼 Gender Analysis

The Gender analysis shows the distribution of employees by gender. It helps users understand the gender composition of the workforce.

## ✈️ Business Travel

The Business Travel analysis shows how frequently employees travel for business purposes. It helps identify employees based on different business-travel categories.

## 💼 Job Role

The Job Role analysis provides employee information across different job roles, including Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales. It helps users understand the workforce distribution across departments and roles.

## 👥 Total Employees

The Total Employees metric displays the overall number of employees included in the selected data and filters.

## 📊 Employee Status

The Employee Status analysis provides an overview of the current status of employees and helps users understand the workforce composition.

## 📈 Attrition Rate

The Attrition Rate shows the percentage of employees who have left the organization compared with the total employee population. It helps monitor employee retention and workforce changes.

## 🎂 Average Age

The Average Age metric displays the average age of employees in the selected data. It provides an overview of the age profile of the workforce.

## 💰 Average Salary

The Average Salary metric shows the average salary of employees based on the selected filters. It helps users understand the general salary level of the workforce.

## ⏳ Average Working Years

The Average Working Years metric shows the average number of years employees have worked in the organization. It provides an overview of employee experience and tenure.

## 🎓 Attrition by Education

The Attrition by Education visualization shows how employee attrition varies across different education levels. It helps identify patterns between educational background and employee retention.

## 💵 Attrition by Salary Slab

The Attrition by Salary Slab visualization shows employee attrition across different salary ranges. It helps users understand whether attrition differs between lower, middle, and higher salary groups.

## 🏢 Attrition by Years at Company

The Attrition by Years at Company visualization shows how attrition varies according to the number of years employees have worked at the organization. It helps identify retention patterns based on employee tenure.

## 🎂 Attrition by Age

The Attrition by Age visualization shows employee attrition across different age groups. It helps identify age-related patterns in employee turnover.

## 😊 Job Satisfaction

The Job Satisfaction analysis shows employee satisfaction levels across the workforce. It helps users understand employee satisfaction and identify patterns that may be associated with workforce retention.

## 💼 Attrition by Job Role

The Attrition by Job Role visualization shows the number or rate of employees leaving across different job roles. It helps identify how attrition varies between Human Resources, Research & Development, Sales, and other available roles.

## 🔎 Interactive Analysis

The HR Dashboard is interactive. When users change slicers such as Marital Status, Gender, Business Travel, or Job Role, the employee metrics and visualizations automatically update according to the selected filters.

## 💡 Key Insights

The Key Insights provided by the dashboard help users understand important workforce patterns, including employee attrition, salary levels, employee age, experience, education, job satisfaction, and job-role distribution.

## 📌 Data-Driven HR Analysis

The HR Dashboard brings employee demographics, salary, experience, job satisfaction, business travel, job roles, and attrition information together in one place. This allows HR teams and decision-makers to explore workforce data and identify important employee trends.
